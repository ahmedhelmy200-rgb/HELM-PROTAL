Cross-Border Claims

International debt recovery and claim coordination

For cross-border claims, the file must identify the debt basis, supporting records, party locations, relevant assets and any prior judgments or proceedings.

Core file components

  • Contract, acknowledgment, invoice or other basis of the claim.
  • Correspondence and supporting evidence.
  • Locations of the parties and relevant assets.
  • Any prior court decision or enforcement-related record.

Multi-jurisdiction approach

We maintain one central file while separating the legal route required in each jurisdiction. Local counsel is engaged where a locally regulated act or court representation is required.

No one-size-fits-all process: the appropriate route depends on the jurisdiction, documents and procedural history.
